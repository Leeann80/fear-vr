# F.E.A.R. VR v0.1.1 — GOG hotfix

This maintenance update for the GOG F.E.A.R. Platinum Collection fixes two
gameplay issues reported after v0.1.0:

- **Ladder progression:** fixes ladder acquisition and catwalk entry, ascent,
  and descent issues that could prevent campaign progress.
- **Pistol support hand:** fixes the support grip drifting away from its
  calibrated position while moving.

## Updating

Quit F.E.A.R., extract the complete **fear-vr-v0.1.1.zip** into a new folder,
and run **F.E.A.R. VR Setup.exe**. Choose **Upgrade F.E.A.R. VR**. You do not
need to uninstall v0.1.0 first. Saves, settings, and original backup lineage
are preserved; modified managed files are saved before replacement.
Do not manually combine files from different archives.

The supported edition for this release is the **GOG base campaign**. The
existing GOG launcher and startup behavior are retained.

Read [Installation](INSTALLATION.md), [Controls](CONTROLS.md), and
[Known limitations](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md). The mod and updates are free.
