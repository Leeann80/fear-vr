# F.E.A.R. VR v0.1.2 — GOG update

- **VR subtitles:** readable dialogue captions below the center of your view. Enable them and adjust Subtitle Size under **Options > VR Settings > Guidance Cards**.
- **Closed-fist melee:** empty hands can strike enemies, glass, and planks with the fingers closed, using the same physical melee behavior as open hands.
- **Independent explosion shake:** **Comfort & Movement > Explosion / Effects Shake** lets you keep explosion/environmental shake while disabling Damage Camera Motion, or turn each off separately.
- **Optional wrist UI:** **Immersion & Interaction > Wrist HUD** hides the wrist display. It defaults to Yes and stays independent of immersion presets.
- **Bad Water progression:** fixes gaze-triggered sequences so they follow where you look in VR.

Includes the ladder progression and pistol support-hand fixes from v0.1.1.

## Updating

Quit F.E.A.R., extract the complete **fear-vr-v0.1.2.zip** into a new folder,
keep **setup-files** beside **F.E.A.R. VR Setup.exe**, then run Setup and choose
**Upgrade F.E.A.R. VR**. No uninstall is needed first. Saves, settings, and
original backup lineage are preserved; modified managed files are saved before
replacement. Do not manually combine files from different archives.

This release supports the **GOG F.E.A.R. Platinum Collection base campaign**.
It retains the existing GOG startup behavior. Other game editions are outside
this release's scope.

Read [Installation](INSTALLATION.md), [Controls](CONTROLS.md), and
[Known limitations](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md). The mod and updates are free.
