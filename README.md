## 🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

### The mod won't start

First, make sure F.E.A.R. is properly installed and has been run at least once before. Also, verify that your VR software (like SteamVR or Meta/Oculus software) is running before you start the mod.

### Controllers not working

Make sure your controllers are paired with your headset and turned on. If they still don't work, try restarting the mod and making sure no other VR applications are running in the background.

### Visual glitches or stuttering

Lower your graphics settings in the F.E.A.R. options menu. VR is more demanding than regular screen play, so you might need to reduce shadows, textures, or resolution. Also, make sure your PC meets the recommended requirements for VR gaming.

### Motion sickness

Start with short play sessions (10-15 minutes) and increase gradually. Use the comfort options in the mod settings, like snap turning and a vignette effect, to reduce nausea.
